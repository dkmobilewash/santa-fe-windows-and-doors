Quieter rooms come with the upgrade

Insulated glass already blocks more sound than a single pane. On the street side, you can go further with glass of two different thicknesses in the same unit, or a laminated pane, both of which dampen traffic noise noticeably.

A tight installation matters just as much. Sound, like air, sneaks in through gaps between the frame and the wall, so we insulate and seal that joint carefully.

Let the trees do some of the work

Where a deciduous tree shades a south or west window, the leaves already block much of the summer sun and drop away just when you want winter warmth. Those windows may not need the strongest solar-control coating, and a glass that lets more heat through in winter can be the better fit.

Unshaded west windows are a different story, and usually get the opposite recommendation.

South Capitol efficiency questions

What's the best glass for a bedroom facing a busy street?

An insulated unit with one laminated pane, or two panes of different thickness, typically gives the biggest improvement in noise without a big jump in cost. We'll bring samples so you can compare.

Do big shade trees change which windows I should buy?

They can. A window well shaded by a deciduous tree in summer may benefit from glass that lets more winter sun through, while an exposed window on the same wall needs stronger heat control.

Can efficient windows go into a historic South Capitol house?

Yes, as long as the style suits the house and any review that applies. Insulated low-E glass is available in wood and clad windows with traditional proportions.
