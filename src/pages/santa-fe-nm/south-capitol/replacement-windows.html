South Capitol replacement windows

Putting the right windows back in South Capitol's older homes

Walk Don Gaspar or Galisteo Street and you'll see houses where every decade left its own window: an original wood double-hung, an aluminum slider from the 1970s, a vinyl unit from a quick flip. Replacing them is a chance to make a South Capitol house look like one house again.

Getting the proportions back

Bungalows and cottages here were designed around windows of a certain shape, often tall double-hungs or several units grouped together on the front. When those were swapped for whatever size fit, the facade lost its rhythm.

We measure the original rough openings, not just the window that's there today. Where an opening was filled in or narrowed during an earlier replacement, we'll show you what restoring it would involve, and whether city review would be part of that.

Styles that suit the neighborhood

Grouped double-hungs

Two or three units joined side by side, a common look on front rooms of older cottages and bungalows. Joined frames can be ordered as one assembly.

Territorial double-hungs

Taller units with divided upper sash, sized to sit under painted wood trim and pedimented heads on Territorial houses.

Casements in stuccoed additions

Later additions and back rooms often suit casements, which seal tightly and open fully on a small wall.

South Capitol window questions

Someone replaced my old windows with smaller ones. Can the original size come back?

Often, yes. If the original opening is still behind the infill, we can open it back up and fit a window of the original size. It does involve wall and trim work, and on the street side it may need review, so we'll walk through the cost and steps first.

Can you match the windows in a rental I own next door?

Yes. We can quote both houses together and use the same style and finish, which keeps the two properties consistent and simplifies ordering.

Are grouped front windows replaced as one unit or separately?

Either way works. Joining them at the factory gives a tidy, strong assembly, while separate units can be easier to carry in and install in a tight space. We'll recommend one based on your opening.
