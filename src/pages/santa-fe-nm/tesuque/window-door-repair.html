Tesuque repair questions

Is there a screen that handles cottonwood fluff better?

A tighter mesh can help keep fluff and small insects out, though it slightly reduces airflow and clarity. We'll show you a sample so you can judge the trade-off.

Can you combine several small repairs into one Tesuque visit?

Yes, and we'd prefer to. List everything in the form or on the phone, and we'll plan the visit so as much as possible gets done at once.

A gust ripped our casement open. Is the window ruined?

Usually not. Bent operators and hinges can be replaced, and we can add a limiter so the sash only opens partway. If the frame itself is cracked, replacement may be the better option.
