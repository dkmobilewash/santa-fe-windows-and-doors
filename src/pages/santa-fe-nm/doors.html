Three doorways, three different problems

Each kind of opening in a Santa Fe house fails in its own way, so each gets a different fix.

The front door

Painted wood entry doors facing south or west bleach, check and pull away from their weatherstripping. A fiberglass door with a wood-grain skin can carry the same painted look with far less upkeep, and a new frame fixes the gap at the latch side.

Curb appeal and security

The portal slider

Sliding doors to the back portal collect fine dust in the bottom track until the rollers grind. Replacement panels with sealed rollers and a better threshold glide again, and a tighter interlock keeps the spring wind on the outside.

Dust, wind and daily use

The courtyard door

Hinged French doors onto an enclosed courtyard suit Territorial and Pueblo-style homes alike. We set them so both leaves meet evenly and the astragal seals, which is where older pairs usually leak.

Light and connection

Getting the finish right in strong sun

Dark paint on a sunny elevation can get hot enough to stress the door it's on. Before you pick a color, we'll look at which way the door faces and how much shade the portal gives it, then recommend a material and finish that will hold up.

If you love the classic blue or turquoise Santa Fe door, it doesn't have to fade in two summers. Factory finishes on fiberglass and clad doors are built for exposure, and wood doors can be protected with an overhang or a storm panel.

Door questions around town

Can I keep the painted look of a traditional Santa Fe front door?

Yes. Fiberglass and clad doors come with factory paint in deep blues, greens and earth tones, and wood doors can be painted any color you like. If your home is in a historic district, color and style may be part of the city's review, and we'll help you check before ordering.

Will a new sliding door actually keep dust out during the spring winds?

A well-built slider with a tight interlock, good weatherstripping and a properly set threshold keeps out far more than a worn one. No door is perfectly dust-proof, but the difference is usually obvious on the first windy afternoon.

My front door opening is arched. Can it be replaced?

Arched and round-top openings can be fitted with made-to-measure doors or a standard door under a fixed arched transom. We'll show you both options and what each costs.

How long does it take to swap an entry door?

A straightforward entry door is usually a single visit, and you have a working, locking door again by the end of that day. Custom sizes and doors with sidelites can take longer, and we'll tell you up front.
