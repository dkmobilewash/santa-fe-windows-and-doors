Homes in Agua Fria

Old village roots, newer neighbors

Agua Fria is one of the area's long-established traditional communities, with roots well before modern Santa Fe spread out to meet it. Along Agua Fria Street and near the river you'll find older adobe homes, many added onto over generations, alongside houses and subdivisions built much more recently.

Part of the area has been brought into Santa Fe city limits while other parts remain in the county, so the rules for your project depend on your exact address. We'll help you find out which applies.

Agua Fria questions

Is my Agua Fria home in the city or the county?

It depends on the address, since parts of Agua Fria have been annexed into Santa Fe and others haven't. We'll help you confirm it early, because it decides which office handles any permits.

Our adobe has rooms built in different decades. Do all the windows need to match?

Not necessarily. Many owners prefer a consistent look on the street side and more flexibility elsewhere. We'll show you options for each part of the house and how they look together.

Can you work around the acequia or gardens next to our house?

Yes. We plan the carry path and staging area during the first visit and protect plantings, ditch banks and walkways during the work.
