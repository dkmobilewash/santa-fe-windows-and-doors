Homes in Rancho Viejo

A planned community on open land

Rancho Viejo spreads across open grassland south of the city, near Santa Fe Community College, with frame-and-stucco homes in Santa Fe style built over the past few decades. Lots look out to the Sangre de Cristos and across wide-open country, which also means plenty of wind and dust.

Many houses still have the windows and patio doors they were built with. Those were often builder-grade units, and seals, rollers and latches are now wearing out.

Rancho Viejo questions

What do we need for Rancho Viejo HOA approval on new windows?

Requirements come from your HOA's guidelines, but submittals usually include the window style, frame color and product information. We prepare those details for you and wait for approval before ordering.

Our windows are only about 20 years old. Is it too early to replace them?

Not necessarily. Builder-grade units often lose their seals and hardware within that time, especially in strong sun. If most are fogged or drafty, replacement may make sense; if only a few are, repair may be enough.

Can we upgrade from vinyl to fiberglass in Rancho Viejo?

Yes, as long as the style and color meet the HOA's guidelines. Fiberglass frames are more stable in heat and cold and come in a range of colors.
