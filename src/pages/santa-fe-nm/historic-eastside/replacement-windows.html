Swapping out tired windows in an Eastside adobe without losing its character

Off Canyon Road, Acequia Madre and Camino del Monte Sol, windows tend to be small, set deep in thick earthen walls, and in many cases decades older than anything a manufacturer still makes. Replacing them here is careful, one-opening-at-a-time work.

Before any window is ordered

Much of the Eastside sits inside the Downtown and Eastside historic district, so new windows on a visible elevation may need city approval first. We confirm that for your address at the start, gather photos and product details for the application, and hold the order until the plan is approved.

For windows the review doesn't cover, such as some rear or interior-courtyard openings, we'll still recommend profiles that sit comfortably next to the rest of the house.

Replacement isn't always the answer here. Sound original sashes can often be repaired instead, and on some historic homes that's the path the city prefers. We'll give you both options when both make sense.

Eastside window replacement questions

Can you replace windows in an Eastside casita or guesthouse too?

Yes. Casitas and guest quarters are often built the same way as the main house, and we can include them in the same quote and review application so the whole property is handled together.

I'm only in Santa Fe part of the year. Can the project happen while I'm away?

It can. We can coordinate access with you or a property manager, send photos at each stage, and schedule a final walk-through for when you're back in town.

Will the new windows sit at the same depth in the wall as the old ones?

That's our default on historic Eastside homes. Keeping the original setback preserves the shadow line on the facade and the deep sill inside, which is a big part of how these houses look.
