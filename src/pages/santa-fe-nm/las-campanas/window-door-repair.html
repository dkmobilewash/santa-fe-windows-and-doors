Repairing oversized glass and heavy doors in Las Campanas homes

When a view window the size of a garage door fogs, or a ten-foot slider stops rolling, the fix calls for more care than an ordinary window repair. Big insulated units are heavy and made to order, and large doors depend on hardware that's carrying a lot of weight.

Replacing a large insulated glass unit

If the frame is sound, a fogged or cracked oversized pane can usually be replaced on its own. We measure the existing unit precisely, order glass made for the altitude with matching coatings so it doesn't look different from its neighbors, and bring enough hands and equipment to set it safely.

On homes facing the golf course, it's a good time to consider laminated glass for that opening.

Getting heavy doors moving again

Large sliders and multi-panel doors wear rollers and tracks faster than small ones, and a little dust on the mesa speeds that along. Replacing rollers, cleaning and straightening tracks, and adjusting panels usually restores easy operation. Big hinged doors that drop at the latch side need hinge or pivot adjustment, and sometimes a frame repair.

Las Campanas repair questions

A golf ball cracked one of our big windows. Does the whole window need replacing?

Usually only the glass does. If the frame and sash are undamaged, we replace the insulated unit and match its coatings to the windows around it.

Will a replacement pane match the tint of our other windows?

We identify the existing glass coating where possible and order a matching one, so the new pane doesn't stand out. If the original can't be identified, we'll show you the closest options before ordering.

Our huge slider takes two hands to move. Can it be fixed?

In most cases, yes. Worn rollers and debris in the track are the usual causes, and both can be dealt with. If the track or frame is damaged, we'll explain what a deeper repair would involve.

Big glass or a heavy door giving you trouble?

Tell us what's happening and send a photo if you can. We'll come out with the right crew and a plan.
