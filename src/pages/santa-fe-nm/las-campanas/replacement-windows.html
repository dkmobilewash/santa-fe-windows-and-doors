Las Campanas replacement windows

Managing a whole-house window replacement in a large Las Campanas home

A custom home out past Camino La Tierra can have forty, sixty or more openings, many of them oversized or shaped, set into finished plaster with stone floors below. Replacing them is less a single installation than a small construction project, and it runs best with a clear plan.

How we plan a large-home project

  1. 1

    Survey and schedule

    Every opening is measured and photographed, and units are grouped by wing or floor so the house never has too many rooms disrupted at once.

  2. 2

    HOA package and ordering

    We prepare product data, colors and elevations for the architectural review, then order once approved, with lead times for custom shapes stated up front.

  3. 3

    Phased installation

    Installation moves wing by wing, with a walkthrough after each phase so issues are caught early rather than at the very end.

Protecting finished interiors

  • Floor protection from the entry to every opening we work on
  • Dust containment around each window while frames come out
  • Art, rugs and furniture moved or covered before work starts in a room
  • Plaster returns repaired and finished to match, not left for others

One look across the whole house

With dozens of units, small differences in frame color, sightlines or grille pattern become obvious. We order everything from one product line where possible and confirm finishes with samples on site, so the north wing matches the south.

Where a home has a few especially large or unusual openings, those can be ordered first so their longer lead times don't hold up the rest of the schedule.

Why Las Campanas owners choose a local crew

Las Campanas window replacement questions

Can we live in the house while all the windows are replaced?

Yes. Phasing the work by wing means only a few rooms are affected at a time, and each opening is closed up again the same day.

How long does a whole-house project take in a home this size?

It depends on the number of openings, how many are custom and any HOA review time. Your quote includes an estimated schedule for ordering and each installation phase.

We're away for part of the year. Can the work happen while the house is empty?

It can. We'll coordinate access with you or your property manager, send progress photos after each phase, and do a final walkthrough with you when you return.
