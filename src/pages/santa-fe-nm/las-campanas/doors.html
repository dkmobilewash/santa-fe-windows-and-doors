Living on a fairway

On lots along the golf course, glass doors facing play can take the occasional hit. Laminated glass holds together when struck, staying in the frame instead of falling into the room, and it also reduces sound and UV. It's worth considering for any large pane in the line of fire.

Open mesa, steady wind

Big doors on exposed elevations need a well-sloped sill, strong interlocks and weatherstripping that keeps sealing as the panels move. We look at exposure and drainage before recommending a configuration, and we'll steer you away from setups that won't hold up on your site.

Las Campanas door questions

Our house backs onto a fairway. What glass should our patio doors have?

Laminated glass is a strong choice for panes facing play. It stays in place if struck and adds sound and UV benefits. We can include it in an insulated unit so efficiency doesn't suffer.

Can a multi-panel door replace our existing sliders without major construction?

Sometimes. If the opening and header can carry it, a new configuration may fit. Widening an opening involves structural work and HOA review, so we'll assess that before recommending anything.

Will a very large door be hard to open every day?

A well-built large door on quality rollers or hinges, installed level and square, should move smoothly. Panel size and weight are part of what we discuss when choosing a configuration.
