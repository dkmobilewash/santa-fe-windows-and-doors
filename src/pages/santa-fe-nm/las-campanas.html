Homes in Las Campanas

Custom homes with big views

Las Campanas, northwest of the city, is a master-planned community of custom homes, many with large windows and doors facing mountain and sunset views.

Big glass brings big challenges: afternoon heat and glare on west-facing walls, UV fading of floors and furnishings, heavy sliding doors that wear over time, and large insulated glass units whose seals can fail.

Las Campanas questions

Do I need HOA approval to replace windows in Las Campanas?

Exterior changes in master-planned communities commonly need HOA architectural approval. Check your HOA's guidelines. We'll provide product details for your submittal and wait for approval before ordering.

Can you replace very large or custom-shaped windows?

Yes. We measure each opening and order units built to size, including arched and other custom shapes. Lead times for custom units vary and are confirmed in your quote.

How do I keep west-facing rooms cooler?

Low-E glass with a lower solar heat gain coefficient blocks more of the sun's heat while letting in daylight. We'll help you choose glass for each side of the house.
