Repairs in Agua Fria

Keeping Agua Fria's older windows and doors in working order

Many families here have kept the same wood windows and doors for generations. With some patient repair work, a lot of them can keep going: a sash reglazed, a sill rebuilt, a door planed and rehung. We start there before talking about replacement.

Wood windows

Dried-out putty lets glass rattle and water creep in. Paint fails first at the bottom rail and sill, where sun and snowmelt hit hardest, and soft wood follows. We remove the failed putty, reset loose panes, cut out rot and splice in new wood where needed, then prime and paint so the repair lasts.

  • Reglazing and loose-pane repair
  • Sill and bottom-rail rebuilds
  • Sashes freed up and made to open again
  • Broken panes replaced, single or insulated

Doors

Adobe walls move a little with the seasons, and doors in them can bind in one season and gap in another. Planing, rehanging on new hinges, moving the strike plate and fitting new weatherstrip usually brings a door back to closing cleanly year-round.

  • Sticking, dragging or swollen doors
  • Sagging hinges and loose screws in old jambs
  • Locks and latches that no longer line up
  • Worn thresholds and missing sweeps

Repair or replace?

Usually worth repairing

Solid old wood with localized rot, loose glass, sticking sashes and doors that just need refitting.

Worth a closer look

Frames with rot through most of the sill, or single panes in rooms that are hard to keep warm.

Time to replace

Aluminum or vinyl units from later additions with broken parts that are no longer made.

Agua Fria repair questions

Can you add insulated glass to our old wood windows?

Sometimes. If the sash is thick enough to take a slim insulated unit, we can retrofit one. If not, an interior storm panel is another way to cut drafts while keeping the original window.

Why does our front door stick only part of the year?

Wood doors and earthen walls both move with changes in moisture and temperature. We fit the door with enough clearance for those changes and seal the gaps with weatherstripping rather than a tight frame.

Will repaired windows look different from the rest?

We match the profile of any new wood to the original and paint to your color, so a repaired sash blends in with its neighbors.
