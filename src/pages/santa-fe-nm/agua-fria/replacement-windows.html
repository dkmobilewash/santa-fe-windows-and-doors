Agua Fria window questions

Can different parts of the house get different windows?

Yes, and in a house built in stages it's often the sensible choice. We keep frame colors and sightlines consistent where they show, and choose whatever fits best where they don't.

Our adobe windows are all slightly different sizes. Is that a problem?

Not for us. Each opening is measured on its own and the units are built to those sizes. It adds a little lead time compared with standard windows.

Do I need a permit to replace windows in Agua Fria?

That depends on whether your home is in the city or the county and on the scope of the work. We check your address and tell you what's required before the order goes in.
