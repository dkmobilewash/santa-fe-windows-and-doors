Built for wind and dust

On an open lot, a door's weatherstrip and sill matter as much as its glass. We look for multi-point locks that pull the panel tight, interlocks that overlap well, and sills that drain. For hinged doors, a closer or stop keeps a sudden gust from wrenching the door against the wall.

We set each door plumb and level on a sealed sill pan, so water and grit that get past the weatherstrip have a way back out.

Rancho Viejo door questions

Can a French door go where my slider is now?

Often, yes, if there's room for the panel to swing and the opening is the right width. We'll check clearance on the portal and inside the room, and tell you whether framing changes are needed.

Why does dust come in under my patio door?

Worn bottom weatherstrip, a tired interlock or a sill that has settled out of level all let dust through. A new door fixes all three, though sometimes new seals and an adjustment are enough.

How long does a door replacement take?

A typical patio or entry door is installed in a single day once it arrives. Lead time on the door itself depends on the style and finish you choose.
