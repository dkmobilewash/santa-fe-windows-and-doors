Fitted to your walls

Doors in thick adobe and stucco walls

Deep walls mean wide jambs, and older openings are rarely perfectly square. We measure carefully, extend jambs where needed, and finish the threshold and trim so the door swings and seals properly.

In a historic district? A new front door may count as an exterior change that needs review. We'll help you find out before anything is ordered.
