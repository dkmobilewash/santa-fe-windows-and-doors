Homes in South Capitol

Older homes near the Capitol

South Capitol, just south of downtown around the State Capitol, is one of Santa Fe's older residential neighborhoods. Homes range from small adobes and cottages to bungalows and Territorial-style houses.

Many still have original wood windows, while others got aluminum or vinyl replacements decades ago. Parts of the neighborhood are inside one of the city's historic districts, so it's worth checking your address before planning exterior changes.

Common projects

  • Replacing worn wood double-hung windows
  • Swapping old aluminum sliders for efficient units
  • Restoring a traditional look with divided lites
  • New front doors that suit the style of the house
  • Repairing sashes, glass and weatherstripping
Historic district? If your home is in a historic district, window and door changes may need city review first. We'll help you find out and prepare; the decision is the city's.

South Capitol questions

Is South Capitol in a historic district?

Parts of it are. Whether review applies depends on your exact address. Choose "Not sure" on our estimate form and we'll help you find out before your consultation.

Can new windows keep my home's traditional look?

Yes. Traditional double-hung and casement styles with divided lites are available, and we can match the proportions of your existing windows.

My windows are original wood. Do they need replacing?

Not always. If frames and sashes are sound, repairs and new weatherstripping may be enough. We'll look at each window and give you honest options.
