Homes in Tesuque

Village adobes and homes in the hills

Tesuque sits along the Tesuque River just north of Santa Fe. The village has older adobe homes with deep openings, and the surrounding hills have custom homes with large windows and long views.

Tesuque is outside Santa Fe city limits, so the city's historic-district review generally doesn't apply. County rules and any HOA or covenant requirements may, and we'll help you check what applies to your property.

Tesuque questions

Does the Santa Fe H-Board review projects in Tesuque?

Tesuque is outside Santa Fe city limits, so the city's historic-district review generally doesn't apply. Santa Fe County rules and any HOA or covenant requirements may. We'll help you check what applies to your property.

Can you fit new windows into old adobe walls?

Yes. We measure each opening individually and plan the window position, sill, jamb extensions and plaster finish so the new window fits properly.

Do you travel to Tesuque for free estimates?

Yes. The in-home consultation and measurement is free in Tesuque, just like in Santa Fe.
