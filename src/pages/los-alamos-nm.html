Homes in Los Alamos

Early town houses and newer mesa homes

Much of Los Alamos grew quickly in the decades after the 1940s, and many neighborhoods still have compact houses from that era, often with original or early replacement windows that are long past their prime. Mesas like North Mesa and Barranca Mesa, and areas such as Quemazon, add newer and larger homes with big openings toward the Jemez Mountains and the Sangre de Cristos across the valley.

Window and door work here falls under Los Alamos County rather than the City of Santa Fe, and some subdivisions have their own covenants. We'll help you check what applies before ordering.

What we plan for

Planning a Los Alamos project

Long, cold winters

Higher and snowier than Santa Fe, Los Alamos rewards windows with a low U-factor, warm-edge spacers and frames that stay tight through freeze-thaw cycles.

Wildfire awareness

The community knows wildfire well. Dual-pane units with tempered glass resist heat better than older glazing and are commonly recommended for homes near forest. No window is fireproof, but it's a sensible upgrade.

Scheduling the drive

Los Alamos is under an hour from our Santa Fe base. We group visits and plan install days around the forecast so snow and ice don't leave an opening exposed.

Los Alamos questions

Do you really come up to Los Alamos for free estimates?

Yes. Los Alamos is part of our regular service area, and the in-home consultation and measurement is free there too.

Who handles permits for window work in Los Alamos?

Los Alamos County, not the City of Santa Fe. Whether a permit is needed depends on the scope, such as changing an opening's size, and we'll look into it as part of your quote.

Can you replace windows in Los Alamos during winter?

Yes, with some planning. We work one opening at a time, watch the forecast, and avoid days when snow or extreme cold would make the work unsafe or leave rooms open too long.

Is tempered glass worth it for a home near the canyon rim or forest?

It's worth considering. Tempered glass in a dual-pane unit holds up to heat far better than older single-pane or untempered glass. We'll price it alongside standard glass so you can compare.
