Repair or replace?

Repair usually makes sense when the frame is solid and square, and the problem is one part: a seal, a balance, a roller, a lock or weatherstripping.

Replacement usually makes sense when frames are rotted, warped or out of square, when several windows have failed seals, or when the windows are single-pane or old aluminum and you want lower energy bills.

Honest advice, either way. We'll look at what's actually wrong and give you the options with prices, so you can decide. Some parts for older or discontinued windows can be hard to find; if that's the case, we'll tell you upfront.
